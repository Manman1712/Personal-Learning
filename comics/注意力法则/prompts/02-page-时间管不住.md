# 02-page-时间与注意力

A clean ligne-claire style illustration, soft and bright palette with warm-white background, teal-green and warm orange accents, modern minimal editorial comic style, even clean linework, no photorealism, Chinese educational comic. Vertical 3:4 portrait composition, split into two clear panels side by side with a subtle arrow between them.

Left panel: The same young East Asian office worker Xiaoyu (short neat hair, light beige shirt) looks flustered, surrounded by a chaotic jumble of alarm clocks, calendars, timers and sticky notes — visual metaphor for fruitlessly "squeezing time". Slightly cluttered, muted.

Right panel: Xiaoyu looks calm and focused, carefully placing a single glowing light-point (representing attention) into a clean transparent hourglass. Clear, orderly, calm.

Text in image (Chinese, clean readable lettering): "你管不住时间，只能决定注意力放在哪". Keep text minimal and well-spaced.

Consistency: same character face, hairstyle, clothing, body proportions as the cover.

Mood: contrast between chaos and clarity. High quality, crisp lines, gentle colors.
